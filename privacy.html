Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Ryku ("we") is a fitness app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Short version: we only keep what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Your training profile: display name, avatar choice, goal, experience level, equipment and body weight you enter.
  • Your workouts and progress: exercises, sets, reps, weights, durations, XP, levels, streaks, badges, quests and settings.
  • Account details: a random account ID. If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your Google account email, name and a Google user ID. We never see your Google password.
  • Social features: your public card (display name, avatar, level, Power Level, tier, streak, weekly XP and friend code), the friends you add and the duels you start.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, advertising IDs or health-platform data, and the app contains no ads or third-party analytics.

How we use it

To run the app: save and back up your progress, sync it between your devices, show leaderboards, friends and duels, and calculate XP, records and calorie estimates. Calorie numbers are estimates, not medical advice.

Who can see it

Your workouts and settings are private to your account. Your public card is visible to other signed-in Ryku players on leaderboards and to friends who add your code. If you share a receipt, card or recap, you choose where it goes.

Where it is stored

Your data is saved on your device and backed up to our database provider, Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India), protected by row-level security so each player can only access their own private data. Google provides sign-in if you choose it. We do not sell or rent personal data.

When you tap Watch a video, the app plays that YouTube video with YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube then receives what any video player sends it, such as your IP address, and its privacy policy applies to that video. Nothing from your Ryku account is sent to YouTube.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete it any time in Settings → Account & sync → Delete: this permanently removes your account, backup, public card, friends and duels. You can also email us to request deletion or a copy of your data.

Children

Ryku is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited by database security rules and server-side checks. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.
