Delete your Ryku account

Ryku: Gym & Home Workout RPG, by YSJ_Devs

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Ryku and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account & sync.
  3. Tap Delete and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email yuvrajjakhar07@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Ryku account", from the Google account email you signed in with. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its sign-in link to Google.
  • Your cloud backup: profile, workouts, progress, plans and settings.
  • Your public card, friends list and duels.

We keep nothing after deletion. Workouts saved on your phone stay on your phone until you uninstall Ryku. If you never signed in with Google, nothing was uploaded, so uninstalling the app removes everything.
